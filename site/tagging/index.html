How each item is tagged

Every program publishes its findings in one shared format, so an item from any layer can be read and filtered the same way on COP ↗. These are the tags, where each comes from, and what it does not claim.

What the tags do not claim

  • Most items are measurements or reports. They carry no likelihood, because they are not judgments. Vis's forecasts carry a likelihood and a confidence; Undertow's items carry the confidence of the assessment they report.
  • A tag says where an item fits. It is not a finding that the item matters, and an unusual reading is not a warning.
  • DIME is not a tag. Items are not sorted by instrument of national power.
