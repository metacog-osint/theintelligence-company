          About this

          What it is

          A free planner for veterans and service members in Fargo-Moorhead on Veterans Day, Wednesday 11 November 2026. It suggests where to go next from where you are, checks you can get there before each deal ends, and learns from what you skip and collect. Veterans who use it share whether each deal was honoured, so everyone sees what's working.

          Method

          Offers come from each business's own 2026 announcements where we found them, and otherwise from 2026 deal lists (Military.com, Stars and Stripes and others) and local news. Every deal shows its sources and a grade: confirmed (the business said so for 2026), announced (2026 deal lists, often "participating locations") or not confirmed (only earlier years; call first). Store locations are checked against the chains' store pages and OpenStreetMap. Drive times are worked out ahead of time on the road network (OSRM, from OpenStreetMap) from a grid of points across the metro to every store, so your phone can time your trip without sending your location anywhere.

          Limits

          Chains let each store decide, and deals change on the day. A deal here is not a promise: when it matters, call ahead. Drive times are estimates for normal traffic. The app doesn't know about lines until someone reports one.

          Your privacy

          No accounts, analytics or tracking. Your location, settings and history stay on your phone. A report you send (for example "worked at this store") carries only the store, the outcome and the time, with no name or ID. To stop the same phone sending floods of reports, the server keeps a scrambled form of your network address for a day, and then deletes it. The map's background tiles come from the OpenStreetMap Foundation, which, like any online map, sees which area you are looking at.
