## Time zone (Q9)
All of Morton County is Central. Wikipedia "Time in North Dakota": "On October 26, 2003 all of Morton County moved to Central Time"; Mountain-time counties are Adams, Billings, Bowman, Golden Valley, Grant, Hettinger, Slope, Stark, plus parts of Dunn, McKenzie and Sioux. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_North_Dakota . Primary rule: 49 CFR 71.7 (eCFR, read 9 Nov 2026) puts the Central/Mountain line along Morton County's southwestern boundary, not through its towns, so Mandan, New Salem, Glen Ullin and Hebron (all in Morton County) are Central. The IANA zone America/North_Dakota/New_Salem exists for the 2003-era area. Caveat: I did not find a page that names Hebron or Flasher specifically (Flasher is in Morton County too). Medora (TRNP) is Mountain.
