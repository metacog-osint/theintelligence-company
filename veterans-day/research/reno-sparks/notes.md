# Reno-Sparks (`reno-sparks`) research notes, 11 Nov 2026

`Downloads\Veterans Day 2026 in Reno–Sparks_ Verified Deals, Freebies and Events for Nov. 11.md` (174 lines, written
about 10:20 PM CST 10 Nov). The report was treated as leads: each offer was opened at its own source on 11 Nov 2026

`tz_abbr` PST, `day_start` 08:00 UTC.
