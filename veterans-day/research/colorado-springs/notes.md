# Colorado Springs, Monument & Falcon (`colorado-springs`) research notes, 11 Nov 2026

10 Nov 2026: an area for colleagues around Colorado Springs (both bases' sides) plus Monument, Palmer Lake and
Falcon, from his research report of that evening (`Veterans-Day-2026-Deals-Colorado-Springs.md`). Built without the publisher on
the night of 10-11 Nov (he asked for research, build and deploy without waiting for his yes). Every local item was
opened at its own source that night (10:55 PM-11:30 PM MST on 10 Nov); the chains are the shared Colorado chain pass
done by the Denver session. Times are Mountain (MST, UTC-7).

## Area
Circles: Downtown and central Springs 6 km, North Springs and the Academy side 6, East side and Peterson SFB 6, South
side and Fort Carson 6, Monument and Palmer Lake 5, Falcon 4, and two added so the whole city is covered (the publisher's
choice): Northgate, Polaris Pointe and Interquest 4 (it also brings in Nana's Dim Sum and Bass Pro), and the Powers
corridor and Stetson Hills 5. Drive times start from downtown without a location. Start presets: downtown, North
Springs (Chapel Hills), the east side on Powers Blvd by Peterson SFB, Fort Carson's Gate 1, Monument, Falcon.

## On the route (local)
- **Memorial Park wreath-laying and observance** (B): 11 AM in the monuments garden at Memorial Park, 1605 E Pikes Peak
  Ave, held by the Pikes Peak Veterans Council and Special Forces Association Chapter 4-24; Mayor Mobolade speaks;
  about an hour, rain or shine, bring a chair. The Gazette (10 Nov 2026) gives the time, hosts and length but doesn't
  call it a wreath-laying; KRDO (1 Sep 2026) does. No city or Council page for 2026 was found. Pin: the park's street
  address (the garden's own coordinates weren't found). `arrive_by` 11:00.
- **Tri-Lakes Post 9-11 memorial, Palmer Lake** (B): 10 AM at the MSG William J. Crawford Memorial (The Gazette, 10 Nov
  2026, its only source). No street address or end time; pin from OpenStreetMap's point for the memorial at the Palmer
  Lake Regional Recreation Area (39.1255, -104.9096), inside the Monument circle. `arrive_by` 10:00.
- **Pikes Peak State College wreath-laying** (A): its own Veterans Day page: "November 11 | 11:00am", Centennial Campus
  Veterans Memorial Courtyard, 5675 S Academy Blvd, in its Veterans Week; Field of Flags at every campus 6-16 Nov. The
  page doesn't say whether the public may attend, so it is listed for veterans, active duty and Guard with a caution
  to ask the MVP office. The Gazette lists it without a day.
- **Texas T-Bone Steakhouse and Famous Dave's, 5245 N Academy Blvd** (B): FOX21's Living Local segment (9 Nov 2026,
  read from its feed; the page returns 403 to the fetch tools): a free Georgia Chopped Pork Sandwich on 11 Nov for
  active-duty, inactive and retired military, dine-in or takeout, with ID (eligibility from a search excerpt). Hours
  from listings, so the card says call first. The national Famous Dave's entry (a two-meat lunch combo, from national
  lists) drops this store (`drop` in CURATE), so the store isn't routed twice with two different offers.
- **Nana's Dim Sum & Dumplings, 1895 Democracy Point** (B): FOX21 Living Local (10 Nov 2026, from its feed): a free
  dine-in meal for every veteran, up to $70 of food, with a military or veteran ID, at participating locations (its
  only Springs store; the chain is Denver's). Routed at lunch, 11 AM-2:30 PM (closed 2:30-4; dinner 4-9:30 also
  counts). Suite 120 per most listings; one Gazette column says 102.

## Under Deals (extras): other days, region-wide, on base or outside the circles
- Pikes Peak Highway (A, City of Colorado Springs news, 9 Nov 2026): free on 11 Nov for **active and retired military**
  with ID, weather permitting. The report's "veterans" rests on the city's older discount page, which couldn't be
  opened; the 2026 page names active and retired only.
- Colorado state parks (A, CPW's 2026 page): free entry for veterans, active duty and Guard, 8 AM-10 PM. NPS fee-free
  day (A; US citizens and residents). Forest Service fee-free day (A; Pike National Forest day-use sites).
- Salute from the Springs (A, the In Their Honor parade's own site; KRDO 1 Sep, Gazette 4 Nov): 1,111 flags downtown
  through 11 Nov. Not routed: no 2026 flag site is published (2025's were S Cascade and N and S Nevada Aves), and the
  parade ran Sat 7 Nov.
- On base or outside the circles: USAFA Planetarium screenings 11, 1 and 3 (base-access pass for non-DoD visitors),
  Air Force basketball, the UCCS Cops & Vets Mile Walk (11 AM; no 2026 location), American Legion Post 38 in Fountain
  (11 AM, 0.3 km outside the South circle), The Classical Academy assembly, Jenkins MS bench dedication, District 11 and
  12 school programs, Woodland Park and the Teller County dinner.
- Bass Pro breakfast 9-11 AM (Mile High on the Cheap, 10 Nov): left here only if the shared Bass Pro chain entry doesn't
  route the Interquest store (see the README).
- U.S. Olympic & Paralympic Museum: **closed Wednesdays**; 20% off for veterans all November (its own post, 5 Nov
  2026), so from Thu 12 Nov.

## Mountain Metro
Mountain Metro itself says nothing about Veterans Day fares: no notice on its pages or news list (latest item 1 Sep
2026), and the city's closures page lists only its administrative offices. Its holiday rule is service by the day of
the week, so regular Wednesday buses. The bar cards' transit line is left off (`transit=None`), as the brief said.

## What's closed on 11 Nov (City of Colorado Springs, 9 Nov 2026)
City offices, Municipal Court, Mountain Metro's administrative offices, the Pioneers Museum, the Deerfield Hills,
Hillside and Westside community centers, the Helen Hunt Falls and Starsmore visitor centers, and the Parks office; Rock
Ledge Ranch's buildings (grounds open). Open: Fairview and Evergreen cemeteries, Patty Jewett and Valley Hi golf, the
Sertich Ice Center, Pikes Peak Highway. The Space Foundation Discovery Center is closed for renovation until January
2027 (its own page).

## Method
Pins: US Census geocoder for street addresses, OpenStreetMap for the Crawford Memorial. FOX21's pages return 403 to
the fetch tools; its RSS feed (`fox21news.com/feed/?s=...`) gave each story's date and opening lines. A research
subagent did the first pass; the Memorial Park, Pikes Peak State College, Texas T-Bone and Nana's sources were opened
again before building.
