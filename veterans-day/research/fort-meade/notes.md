  DoD-issued ID (Armed Forces, Uniformed Services or Military Retiree), a VA-issued ID, or an MVA driver's license or

## Corrections to the research report
1. **MTA ID:** the report's Tips line says MTA accepts "the first three" of "DoD ID, VA ID, DD-214 or MVA
   veteran-designated license", which would include the DD-214. MTA's alert lists a DoD ID, a VA ID or the MVA Veteran
   designation; a DD-214 is not on it. (The report's own table had it right.) Its "eighth consecutive year" wording
   isn't in the alert text read here (it may be in MTA's 9 Nov release, not read).
2. **Red Robin:** not at 1403 S Main Chapel Way, Gambrills (the report's OpenTable "Red Robin - Crofton" page). Red
   Robin's own list (17 Oct 2026 scrape) has no store there and OpenStreetMap has none; the area's one Red Robin is
   Columbia Crossing, 8640 Snowden River Pkwy.
3. **Bob Evans:** not in Gambrills or Laurel. Bob Evans' own list (31 Oct 2026) has one store in the circles:
   Columbia, 8325 Benson Dr. OpenStreetMap's Laurel store (3361 Corridor Marketplace) isn't on the chain's list and is
   left out as likely closed.
4. **Outback:** Laurel too (14718 Baltimore Ave, on Outback's own list), not only Gambrills.
5. **Red Lobster, Arundel Mills:** on Red Lobster's own store list (7063 Arundel Mills Blvd), not only a third-party
   listing. The offer itself stays B with "not announced by the company for 2026".
6. **Great Clips:** not "many salons" in Odenton, Columbia and Laurel. Great Clips' own list has two inside the
   circles, Odenton (8769 Piney Orchard Pkwy) and Ellicott City (4725 Dorsey Hall Dr), and none in Laurel; Severn
   (Quarterfield Rd) and Elkridge (Marshalee Dr) are just outside.
7. **Mission BBQ and Shake Shack "Confirmed 2026":** no 2026 post from either company was found; both stay B in the
   planner (Military.com 8 Nov; American Legion 5 Nov; WTOP 9 Nov has Mission BBQ's "Free sandwich and cake").
8. **Silver Diner Elkridge "outside core":** with these circles it's inside (Columbia circle), so it's on the route.
9. **Howard County:** the report has Robinson Nature Center open; Howard County's own Veterans Day notice found was
   2024's, which closed it. No 2026 notice was found, so the page doesn't say it's open. The report's library closure
   (HCLS's page refused automated reads) and open schools are unconfirmed and the page says so.
10. **Prince George's County closures missing:** most of Laurel is in Prince George's County, where county offices,
    public schools and libraries are closed (WTOP, 10 Nov 2026).
11. **Fort Meade MWR hours:** the report couldn't retrieve 2026's. DFMWR's sheet (as of 17 Aug 2026, linked from the
    26 Oct 2026 notice): Bowling Center noon-4 PM (the report's 2022 hours were 10-9), Gaffney Fitness Center 8 AM-noon,
    Murphy Field House 24/7 for registered users, everything else closed.
12. **Garrison command:** not "interim leadership". Col. Joshua Patton took command on 15 Oct 2026 at Club Meade
    (army.mil, 19 Oct 2026); Deputy to the Garrison Commander Brian Foley led in between. (Not on the page.)
13. **National Cryptologic Museum:** the report says there's no Nov 11 closure. NSA's site refuses automated reads
    here, and the coordinating session's search summary of NSA's pages says it closes on federal holidays, so it's
    listed (C) with "call (301) 688-5849", not routed.
14. **The Greene Turtle:** has a Veterans Day offer this year (WTOP, above), not only its everyday 15%.
15. **Grace for Vets:** the report said to check the site; its list has Flagship Carwash, Severn, in the area.
