## Metro Transit
No general free rides for veterans on Veterans Day found. Only service-connected disabled veterans ride free at all times with a VA ID marked "Service Connected" or "SC". Source: https://www.metrotransit.org/fares (read 9 Nov 2026). Regular holiday schedule on 11 Nov per Bring Me The News, 8 Nov 2026: https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/whats-open-whats-closed-minnesota-133457325.html . Other "Metro" systems (Cincinnati, Topeka, Las Vegas RTC) do offer free rides; those are not Minnesota.
