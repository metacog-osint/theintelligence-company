# Colorado differences for chains already in national-2026.json

Checked 2026-11-11 (early morning Central). These chains stay in `national-2026.json`; the notes below cover only what differs in the Denver metro and Colorado Springs.

## Jiffy Lube: free oil change at Griffin Fast Lube shops

- The national entry says some franchisees give 50% off. In Colorado, franchisee Griffin Fast Lube gives veterans and active military a **free conventional oil change (up to 5 quarts)** on Wed 11 Nov 2026, at participating Denver-metro shops (about 20) and Colorado Springs shops.
- Extra charges: fees may apply for 4x4s, shop supplies, extra oil and special filters; tax not included. Proper military ID required. Participation varies by shop, so call ahead. Hours not stated.
- Shop lists: https://www.jiffylubegfl.com/denver-metro-locations and https://www.jiffylubegfl.com/colorado-springs-locations. The franchise's own Denver page carried no Veterans Day text when read.
- Sources: Mile High on the Cheap, "Free oil change at Jiffy Lube on Veterans Day", posted and updated 2026-11-10, https://milehighonthecheap.com/free-oil-change-jiffy-lube-veterans-day-denver ; Mile High on the Cheap main list, updated 2026-11-10, https://www.milehighonthecheap.com/veterans-day-deals-freebies-denver/ ; KOOL 105, 2026-11-10, https://www.kool105.com/2026/11/10/veterans-day-deals-in-metro-denver/ . Grade B.

## Cracker Barrel: Colorado stores and Old Country Store discount

- Same free Sunrise Pancake Special (dine-in, proof of service, while supplies last). Denver-metro stores named: Denver (Central Park) and Northglenn; Colorado Springs also has a store.
- Colorado lists add **25% off the military collection in the Old Country Store** on 11 Nov, which the national entry does not mention.
- Sources: Mile High on the Cheap (2026-11-10), KOOL 105 (2026-11-10), Deseret News (2026-11-10, citing Cracker Barrel's Instagram), https://www.deseret.com/lifestyle/2026/11/10/veterans-day-2026-where-to-get-free-meals-discounts/ . Grade B.

## Pilot / Flying J: Colorado travel centers

- Same offer: free sandwich plus coffee or fountain drink through the Pilot app after ID.me verification (verify before going).
- Colorado sites named: Aurora and Limon (Mile High on the Cheap); Aurora, Brighton and Hudson (KOOL 105). Participating sites only. No Pilot site in Colorado Springs proper was named by the Denver lists; the Springs research report mentions a "Springs travel center" without an address.
- Sources: Mile High on the Cheap (2026-11-10), KOOL 105 (2026-11-10). Grade B.
